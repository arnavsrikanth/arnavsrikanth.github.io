Hip Exoskeleton

SolidWorks 3D Printing PCB Design Python

The hip exoskeleton is the main project at MetaMobility Lab. I've worked on a few key hardware related upgrades to it as well as some more hardware and software related projects currently in the pipeline.

Junction PCB Upgrade

Originally our hip exoskeleton had a bundle of wires soldered and glued together carrying 24V power and CAN communications to our motors as well as IMU and encoder data back to the brains of the project: an Nvidia Jetson Orin Nano. As is apparent, the bundle of wires held together by solder, hot glue, and the tears of grad students is not the safest nor the most sturdy solution. Below is a PCB I designed incorporating a multiplexer for our 3 IMU data streams as well as two CAN communications streams, a chip mounted IMU, and 24V power delivery for our motors. These all bundle into a single eight wire cable that runs back to our Jetson and main power delivery board.
