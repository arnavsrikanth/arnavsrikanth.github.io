Smartville 360

SolidWorks Sheet Metal Mfg Cable Harness Design Design for Manufacturing

This was a stationary battery electric storage system designed as the flagship project of Smartville. It repurposed end of life Tesla Model 3 EV batteries as stationary storage to support commercial and utility applications.

Why repurpose used EV batteries?

We all grew up learning about reduce, reuse, recycle. And as you know, it's important to make sure to reuse things before we recycle. This also applies to EV batteries. As anyone who owns an electric vehicle can tell you, EV batteries are not cheap. Largely due to the fact that they are full of rare earth metals. And due to the lack of recycling capacity in the US, many EV batteries are shipped outside the US for recycling. Effectively surrendering these valuable metals. Our plan at Smartville was to repurpose these used batteries (which can have up to 75% capacity still left at end of life!) into stationary storage. This would save tons of money on shipping and recycling costs as well as keep rare earth metals within the US. In addition, it would bolster electrical storage capacity at industrial sites during outages as well as allow them to store excess solar power generated throughout the day for use during high cost hours in the evenings.

Mechanical Hardware Contributions

I designed sheet metal manufactured enclosures for our electrical and controls subsystems such as Battery Interface Box (ensured equal voltage between battery pairs), HVJB, and system controller. Each subsystem's enclosure was designed to fit in a standard server rack that we had installed in the Smartville 360 for ease of access as well as maintenance.

Cable Harness Design Contributions

I designed and sourced components for all high low voltage cable harnesses on the system. I also created documentation for manufacturing of cable harnesses. In addition, I manufactured prototype harnesses and tested them for fit, durability, and electrical safety.
